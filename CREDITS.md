# Image credits

All photography is stored locally in `public/images` (nothing is hot-linked) and
was colour-graded with the Cocoya look (`scripts/images.mjs`). Each image's licence
allows commercial use and modification; attribution is not required for any of
them, but it is listed here as good practice.

## Cocoya's own photography

Everything in `public/images/cocoya/` (hero, story, gallery, signature drinks,
specials, visit and admin imagery) is **Cocoya's own photography**, supplied by
the café and gently enhanced by `scripts/cocoya-photos.mjs`. Originals are kept in
`photos/cocoya-originals/`. © Cocoya Coffee House — not for reuse.
